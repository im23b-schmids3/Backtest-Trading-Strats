# Phase E portfolio review roles

These role prompts are intentionally evidence-bound. They may summarize persisted
portfolio metrics, overlap, correlation, shared-account risk, prop economics,
ablation, and stress results. They must not run backtests, optimize parameters,
inspect untouched holdout data, or change portfolio membership.

The Python registry and artifacts are the source of truth. A role must cite the
registry table or artifact that supports each material observation and must report
insufficient evidence instead of guessing.
