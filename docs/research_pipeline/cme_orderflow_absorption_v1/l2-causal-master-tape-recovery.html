Windows publish root cause

The worker-pool hypothesis was rejected: the original with ProcessPoolExecutor(...) block completed shutdown(wait=True) before artifact combination, three reproduction passes, report emission, and the final rename. The final destination did not exist, its parent ACL allowed modification, and a normal same-directory Path.replace probe succeeded.

The defect was nondeterministic reader-handle lifetime. _iter_parquet_rows() created pyarrow.parquet.ParquetFile objects without an explicit close(), and _read_parquet_rows() did not own an explicit reader lifecycle. On Windows, an open PyArrow Parquet reader prevents renaming its containing directory. A controlled local probe reproduced the exact failure as [WinError 5] Zugriff verweigert while the reader was open. The original process attempted its final directory rename without a deterministic all-readers-closed barrier.

The repair explicitly closes every Parquet reader in finally, closes early-terminated iterators, explicitly joins the process pool with shutdown(wait=True), validates the complete staging tree, releases wrapper cycles, and then uses a same-parent os.rename. The unchanged 1.0 GB tree published successfully through that path.
