## Recovered historical contract (code evidence only)

V6 uses `StrategyV6PostTP1StopResearch`; V6.5 inherits it and changes only named post-TP1 stop placement. The fixed entry is Fib 0.900. For a range `low < high`, long price at ratio `r` is `high-r*(high-low)`; short price is `low+r*(high-low)`. Initial stop is ratio 1.02. Profile B partially exits at ratios 0.786/0.618/0.500/0.236/0.050 with original-position fractions 30%/25%/20%/15%/10%. After TP1, V6 policy C is 0.786; V6.5 exposes 0.900 through 0.820 and 0.786, plus no movement. A moved stop is active only on the next candle; no movement retains 1.02.

Long lifecycle: begin with the first low/high; maintain active low anchors, update every active setup on a higher high, and create a new anchor from a provisional higher low only when the later favorable high is at least `min_distance` bars away and reaches `min_move`. Invalidate when a later low is below its anchor or anchor age exceeds the timeframe limit. Short is the mirrored high/low construction. Events are calculated at candle close and orders activate from the next indexed candle. `min_distance` is a bar-count filter; `min_move` is favorable excursion divided by the anchor/candidate price. V6’s grid source is `DISTANCES`/`MOVES` imported from V2; their actual values were not independently recovered in this audit and must be authenticated from the generator source in a future implementation review.

An order fills if long low is at/below the limit or short high is at/above it. Fill price applies adverse slippage. At each bar V2 processes eligible orders, then positions, then emits events. For a position, conservative execution closes the full remaining amount at the stop before targets if both are reachable. Otherwise it processes targets in index order. A V6 stop newly moved by TP1 is not rechecked in that TP1 bar. The final remaining quantity is force-closed at the final available close with `end_of_test`.

Sizing is compounded: risk budget is 2% of marked equity, quantity is `risk_budget / abs(slipped_entry - raw_initial_stop)`. Entry fees are immediately deducted; exit fees are deducted for each partial/full exit. Both entry and every exit use adverse slippage; reported slippage cost includes entry and exit deviation times quantity. Position limits additionally use `max_positions`, reserved-notional versus `equity*leverage`, and planned risk versus `equity*max_total_risk_fraction`. V6/V6.5 set `max_positions=1` per evaluated asset/timeframe. Default config identifies BTC/ETH as Binance `BTC/USDT` and `ETH/USDT`, fee 0.001 and slippage 0.0002, UTC timestamps, initial cash 10,000, conservative execution, and maximum anchor ages 4h=60 days/1d=180 days. Code validates absent bars as errors unless expected gaps are explicitly allowed; no original data-validation result, missing-bar record, exact cache identity, data hash, start/end timestamp, or session calendar was available.

## Ranking, chronology, and no-lookahead limits

V6.5 selects one V6 policy-C row per non-Gold asset/timeframe after `number_of_trades >= 20`, sorted by asset/timeframe then V6 robust score descending/rank ascending. It varies its 11 post-TP1 placements. Its robust score is `tanh(total_return/.5) - .75*abs(maximum_drawdown) + clip(sharpe,-3,3)/12 + (clip(PF,0,3)-1)/12 - stop_neighborhood_penalty - placement_instability_penalty - consistency_penalty - cost_penalty - low_trade_penalty`. The penalties are respectively deviation from per-configuration stop median plus standard deviation; `tanh` of per-policy return standard deviation times .35; `tanh(abs(early_return-late_return))*.20`; clipped costs/gross-PnL ratio times .10; and `.75*max(0,20-trades)/20`. `early_return` and `late_return` split the equity series at its midpoint index.

V6 ranking has analogous but different penalties: low trades below 30, nearby parameter deviation/standard deviation, per-policy instability, raw early/late difference times .10, asset/timeframe concentration, and cost. V6 has an internal walk-forward that reserves final 365 days, but V6.5 itself consumes a V6 full-matrix selection and has no independent holdout in this source. This creates chronology and reuse concerns that cannot be resolved without original generated metadata/matrices. The code and a unit test support causal pivot confirmation and next-bar activation, but they do not prove the original historical run used the inspected revision, original data, a complete no-lookahead reconciliation, or an intrabar path beyond conservative stop precedence.
