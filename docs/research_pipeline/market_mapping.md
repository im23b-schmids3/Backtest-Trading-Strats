# Market mapping

Mappings are typed and hashed. BTCUSDT/ETHUSDT map to MBT/MET respectively;
PAXGUSDT, XAGUSDT, SPX, and QQQ are explicitly proxy mappings to MGC, SIL, MES,
and MNQ. Native/proxy labels, limitations, confidence, date range, source
symbol, transformation, and duplicate-exposure group are required.
